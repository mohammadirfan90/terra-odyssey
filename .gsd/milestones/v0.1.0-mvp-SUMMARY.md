## Lessons Learned & Key Decisions
1. **Separation of Operational and Scientific Status**: Decoupling `job_status="succeeded"` from `result_status="inconclusive"` is essential so scientific non-detections or ineligible time spans do not trigger operational server failure alarms.
2. **No Viridis for Signed Trends**: Viridis is sequential; signed trends require zero-centred diverging scales (`RdBu`/`BrBG`) with a symmetric domain $[-\max|\beta|, +\max|\beta|]$ frozen across pan and zoom.
3. **Paired Direct Contrast**: Difference series $D_t = Y_{A,t} - Y_{B,t}$ accounts for inter-regional covariance and common climate modes; comparing separate isolated $p$-values is mathematically invalid.
4. **HAC Uncertainty Scope**: Newey-West HAC provides uncertainty for regression slopes, not year-by-year error envelopes around raw difference points.
5. **Autocorrelation Awareness**: Standard Mann-Kendall test inflates false positives under positive serial dependence; Hamed & Rao (1998) variance correction or OLS + Newey-West HAC prevents false discoveries.
