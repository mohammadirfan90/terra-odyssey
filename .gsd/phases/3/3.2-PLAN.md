# Plan 3.2: Paired Regional Contrast Estimator & HAC Difference Testing Engine

## Objective
Implement and verify the paired regional difference contrast estimator ($H_0: \beta_A - \beta_B = 0$). Strictly enforce scientific rules: align Region A and Region B on identical calendar years, require at least 20 consecutive common complete years, compute synchronous difference series $\Delta_t = y_{A,t} - y_{B,t}$, estimate contrast slope $\beta_\Delta$ with Newey-West HAC uncertainty, and classify evidence status honestly without manufacturing opposite trends.
